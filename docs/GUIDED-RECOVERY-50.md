Recovery copies are plaintext browser storage, not a backup guarantee. Avoid shared devices and export important work. Character/scene resources, uploaded media, generated assets and server history are not bundled in a studio draft. Imports never change destination identity or save automatically. Existing game recovery remains separate.

The Help control includes searchable field explanations and examples. Examples do not populate forms. Music receives guidance in this batch; its existing history and save protection remain in place.
